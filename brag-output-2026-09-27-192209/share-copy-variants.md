# Share Copy Variants: Photobooth Station

### 1. Twitter / X (Short & Punchy)
We built a retro photobooth station, but the camera became a living creature that purrs when petted, eats your photos, and burps cartoon bubbles. 📸👾💛

Type "cheese" for a surprise.

### 2. LinkedIn (Design & Creative Engineering)
What happens when you treat your webcam like a living creature instead of an input stream?

Photobooth Station is an experiment in playful neo-brutalist interaction design:
• A living creature body that wraps around the 16:9 webcam
• Tactile petting physics (eyes curve into happy arches ^ ^ and purrs)
• Pure synthesized Web Audio sound engine (zero audio files needed)
• Swallowing photo strips into a living memory drawer
• Post-meal belly wiggle and cartoon burp bubbles

Web design doesn't have to be sterile. Feed your camera at photobooth.local.

### 3. Discord / Community (Fun & Casual)
Made a neo-brutalist photobooth where your camera is literally a living creature. Stroke its forehead and it purrs, take a 4-cut photo strip and it swallows it whole in mid-air, then burps a bubble. Also if you type "cheese" it puts on retro pixel sunglasses and throws confetti. 🕶️✨
