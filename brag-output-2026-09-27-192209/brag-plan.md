# Brag Plan: Photobooth Station

## What is this app?
Photobooth Station is a playful, tactile neo-brutalist web photobooth where the camera is a quirky living creature with synthesized audio, petting physics, a voracious appetite for photos, post-meal tummy burps, and secret party shades.

## The angle
"Someone turned the photobooth camera into a living creature that purrs when petted, eats your photos, and burps bubbles."
Instead of an impersonal webcam app or a sterile SaaS video recorder, the entire experience centers on a responsive handmade toy creature hugging the 16:9 portal. It purrs when you stroke its forehead, anticipates your snapshot, gobbles up your 4-cut photo strip in mid-air, burps a cartoon bubble, and parties with pixel sunglasses when you type "cheese".

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The video opens on the creature deep in slumber: eyelids closed into straight horizontal slits with floating curved "Z Z Z" characters bobbing upward. A retro pointer cursor darts onto its forehead and strokes back and forth—instantly the creature enters blissful petting mode: eyes curve into happy arches (`^ ^`), ears flutter, floating heart and sparkle emotes (`💛`, `✨`) pop up, and a sweet purr sounds. Text slams in: **"MEET YOUR WEBCAM."** with subtag: *"(it purrs when you pet it)"*.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Living Viewfinder & Theme Morph:** Creature's paws grip the 16:9 camera frame with tactile claws. A rapid swatch toggle clicks from monochrome to retro pastel, morphing the whole booth instantly while a monocle snaps onto the creature's eye as the vintage filter kicks in.
- **The Shoot & Flash:** User hovers the giant yellow **"TAKE!"** button—the creature leans into the lens with wide anticipation. The winding key on its head whirs like clockwork as a retro countdown overlay ticks: "3... 2... 1...". FLASH! A blinding screen flash causes the creature to wince tight shut (`> <`) and paws clutch inward in recoil.
- **The Feast & Post-Meal Burp:** The fresh 4-cut photo strip flies upward in a parabolic arc. The creature's mouth opens wide into a red chomping maw (`mouth_eating`), swallowing the strip whole in mid-air! 1.5 seconds later, its belly section shakes with a digestive wiggle, mouth opens, and an iridescent cartoon bubble floats up with a comic `*pop*` as paws pat its tummy!
- **Secret Party Mode:** Swallowed strip lands inside Memories. Cursor types "CHEESE"—cool retro pixel sunglasses snap onto the creature's face, accompanied by an 8-bit fanfare and a festive explosion of neo-brutalist confetti!

## Outro / punchline
The creature winks proudly wearing its retro shades. A bold neo-brutalist badge slams down:
**PHOTOBOOTH STATION**
*Retro Snap Studio • 2026*
**"FEED YOUR CAMERA AT PHOTOBOOTH.LOCAL"**

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry & Petting:** Sleeping creature woken up by gentle forehead petting (`^ ^` happy eyes, fluttering ears, floating hearts).
2. **Customization & Shoot:** Pastel theme switch + vintage filter monocle + clicking "TAKE!" with 3s countdown and flash wince.
3. **The Feast & Burp:** Photo swallow → digestive belly wiggle → floating burp bubble.
4. **Secret Party:** Typing "cheese" → retro sunglasses + confetti burst.
